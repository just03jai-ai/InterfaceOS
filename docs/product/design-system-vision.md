# Design System Vision

InterfaceOS is not a component catalogue. It is a governed map of design intent, implementation behavior, evidence, relationships, and change over time.

## Vision

Every product decision should be discoverable by humans, understandable by machines, editable in its authoritative system, connected to downstream implementations, and supported by reviewable evidence.

## Strategic pillars

1. **Design intelligence:** preserve intent and evidence, not screenshots alone.
2. **Implementation intelligence:** connect specifications to coded behavior and tests.
3. **Relationship intelligence:** expose dependencies across tokens, components, patterns, pages, and flows.
4. **Governance intelligence:** make ownership, status, approvals, exceptions, and releases visible.
5. **AI intelligence:** provide scoped, versioned, provenance-bearing context instead of unstructured scraping.

## Non-goals

- Maximizing component count.
- Replacing Figma, GitHub, or Storybook.
- Treating generated AI output as approved truth.
- Centralizing every product decision into an untyped database.

## Horizon

The platform evolves from trusted foundations, to connected artifacts, to searchable relationships, to governed automation. Automation is earned only after canonical contracts and evidence are reliable.
