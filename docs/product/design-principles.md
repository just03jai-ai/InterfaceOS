# Design Principles

1. **Evidence before assertion** — A status or quality claim links to its proof.
2. **Accessible by default** — Accessibility is specified, designed, tested, and reviewed throughout the lifecycle.
3. **Intent survives implementation** — Visual and coded representations preserve the rationale and constraints of the decision.
4. **Tokens express meaning** — Semantic intent precedes raw values and component overrides.
5. **One owner per truth** — Each artifact field has a declared authoritative system and accountable owner.
6. **Progressive disclosure** — Interfaces reveal essential decisions first and supporting depth on demand.
7. **Composable, not generic** — Reuse stable responsibilities without erasing domain meaning.
8. **Machine-readable, human-legible** — Structured data and authored explanation are complementary release outputs.
9. **Change is a product surface** — Versions, migrations, deprecations, and downstream impact are visible.
10. **Automation remains accountable** — AI and tooling expose provenance, constraints, failures, and required approval.

These principles are validation criteria for IOS-001; changes require a recorded decision and governance approval.
